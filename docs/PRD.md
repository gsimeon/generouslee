# Generouslee — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
**Generouslee** is a digital wellbeing, relationships, parenting, motherhood, mentorship, and personal-growth platform. It is designed to transform the founder’s viral social-media and TikTok community into an enduring, trusted, and monetizable digital ecosystem.

## 2. Target Audience & Personas
- **New & Expectant Mothers**: Women experiencing the identity shock of *matrescence*, postpartum overwhelm, sleep deprivation, and maternal guilt.
- **Married Couples with Children**: Spouses navigating "roommate syndrome", uneven division of invisible domestic labor, and communication friction.
- **Conscious Parents**: Parents seeking gentle boundary scripts, nervous system co-regulation methods, and non-punitive guidance for toddler & childhood meltdowns.
- **Overstimulated Women**: Women seeking permission to step out of people-pleasing, establish firm boundaries with in-laws/workplaces, and protect personal downtime.

## 3. Core Product Modules (Phase 1 — MVP)
1. **Public Content Ecosystem**: High-quality editorial articles, practical guides, printable checklists, and TikTok spotlight embeds across 5 flagship pillars: Motherhood, Marriage, Parenting, Mental Wellness, and Personal Growth.
2. **"Ask Generouslee" Signature Service**: Anonymous or identified question submission engine with founder & expert review, moderation, and published wisdom library with actionable takeaways.
3. **Personalized Member Dashboard**: Interest-based recommendations, "Continue Reading", saved article bookmarking, active question status tracking.
4. **Onboarding & Interest Selection**: Multi-select preferences during sign-up to tailor content feed.
5. **Administrative Platform (CMS & Operations)**: RBAC-enforced admin portal for publishing articles, reviewing & answering questions, managing categories/tags, tracking audit logs, and reviewing conversion funnels.
6. **Safety & Crisis Interceptor**: Unambiguous disclaimers affirming Generouslee is educational mentorship, not medical, clinical psychological, or legal advice, with immediate links to emergency and maternal crisis resources.

## 4. Phase 2 & 3 Expansion Roadmap
- **Community Groups**: Moderated safe-space discussion circles (New Mothers, Marriage in the Thick of It, Toddler Parenting).
- **Academy**: Structured video & workbook masterclasses (e.g. *From Overwhelmed to Anchored*, *Marriage After Baby*).
- **Mentorship 1-on-1**: Timezone-aware booking engine for private coaching and group circles with Paystack integration.
- **Paystack Subscriptions**: Tiered membership (Free, Generouslee Plus, Founder Circle).
